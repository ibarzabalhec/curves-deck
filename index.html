A history of construction, from the cave to the printer

Héctor Ibarzábal Rodríguez · Rice MBA 2027

The premise

For most of history, a curve cost labor. The factory made the straight line cheap. A printer lays a curve at the price of a straight wall.

“Unlike conventional construction methods, 3D printing can produce a curved wall for the same cost as a straight one.”

Texas Architect, “Print Preview,” May 2023
